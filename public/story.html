The ritual

IT STARTED WITH A SIMPLE QUESTION.

What makes an incense truly good?

For us, the answer began long before Ananda International.

1960
WE STARTED
WITH THE SOURCE.

Our journey began as raw material suppliers to the incense industry.

From the Himalayan ranges to the Western Ghats and the forests of Southern India, we built relationships with sources of distinctive natural materials and learned what makes each ingredient special.

We learned the source before we learned the product.

1980
WE STARTED
MAKING OUR OWN.

We turned decades of knowledge into our own dhops and agarbattis. Our belief was simple:

  • Natural ingredients.
  • Thoughtful formulations.
  • Fragrances people would remember.

We began in Karnataka with brands such as Panchpeeta and Gandhada Gudi, earning acceptance through the combination of quality and value. From there, our journey took us across India — and eventually beyond it.

FROM INDIA
TO THE WORLD.

Different markets. Different preferences. But one thing remained constant. We kept improving the formulation.

Then international markets, including South America.

THE FORMULA
GOES DEEPER.

Natural resins. Herbal powders. Oils. Aromatic materials. Carefully developed perfumes. Not simply to make incense that smells good in the packet — but to make it perform when it burns.

RESINS · HERBS · OILS · AROMATICS · PERFUME

PUSHING
THE BOUNDARIES.

We kept asking — and creating.

OUR OWN WAY.

When conventional machinery couldn’t deliver what we wanted — we developed our own.

Some formulations are still prepared and finished by hand, using specialised recipes refined through years of experience.

BECAUSE WE ASK
DIFFERENT QUESTIONS.

Not just: “How can we make it cheaper?”

But:

  • Where did the ingredients come from?
  • How will they perform when burned?
  • How long will the fragrance last?
  • Can we make it better without taking shortcuts?

TODAY,
YOU SEE THE PRODUCT.

But behind every Ananda incense is:

60+ YEARS of understanding raw materials.

40+ YEARS of manufacturing experience.

DECADES of formulation and refinement.

ONE BELIEF — That quality should be experienced — not simply claimed.

SO, THE NEXT TIME YOU LIGHT INCENSE...

Don’t just ask: “What does it smell like?”

Ask: “What went into making it?”

We have spent generations answering that question. Now, experience the difference for yourself.
